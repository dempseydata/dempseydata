I've worked at companies of every size, from Series A startups to industry behemoths like Netflix, Capital One and Amazon. I keep landing in orgs where the product practice needs evolving, or I'm building it from scratch. 12 years as a dedicated PM, and several years of business intelligence and analytics at scale before that. I've worked in domains stretching through expense analytics, quarterly financial reports, account-based marketing, call center operations, cloud infrastructure security, digital workplace platforms, epidemic-risk analytics, commercial food supply chain, and now applied AI. I get thrown into domains I don't know yet and figure them out fast, and become the first point of contact for my product or platform. I might not know the specific answer, but I'll know who does.

**Current core tools:** Jira, SQL, Claude Code.
