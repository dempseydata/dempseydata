Product leader who keeps landing in orgs where the product practice needs evolving, or building it from scratch. I've worked at companies of every size, from Series A startups to Netflix, Capital One and Amazon: 12 years as a dedicated PM, after several years of business intelligence and analytics at scale.

My domains range from cloud infrastructure security and account-based marketing to epidemic-risk analytics, and now applied AI. I get thrown into domains I don't know yet and figure them out fast, becoming the primary point of contact for my product or platform. I might not know the specific answer, but I'll know who does.

## Currently building

These repos are my play area: curiosity projects, me geeking out, and learning by building. Maybe someone finds them useful, maybe not.

- [hindsight](https://github.com/dempseydata/hindsight): local observability over Claude Code history, covering what was done, where the tokens went, and how the process ran.
- [third-shift](https://github.com/dempseydata/third-shift): an unattended software factory for Claude Code that works agent-ready tickets, verifies each one, and leaves a handover.
- [dempsey-skills](https://github.com/dempseydata/dempsey-skills): Claude Code skills, including `security-preflight`, a pre-deploy security check for solo-built projects.
